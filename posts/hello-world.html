Hello, world

October 14, 2026 · 2 min read

Welcome to my corner of the internet. This is the first post on a site I built by hand — plain HTML, CSS, and a little JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, nothing to break.

Why a website?

I wanted a place that's fully mine: to show my work, to write about what I learn, and to have something I can shape over the years. Owning the whole thing — domain, design, content — felt like the right move.

What I'll write about

  • Projects I'm building and the decisions behind them
  • Tools and workflows I find useful
  • Lessons learned the slow way, so you don't have to

What's next

I'll be filling out the projects section and writing as I go. If you have feedback or just want to say hi, the contact page is one click away.
